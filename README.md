# Listing Collector for Etsy

A free Chrome extension for Etsy research. While you browse Etsy, it puts three small save buttons under every
listing. Click one and that listing goes into one of three lists. Then download the list as a spreadsheet or
copy it into Google Sheets.

## Install it

It works in Google Chrome and in browsers built on Chrome, like Microsoft Edge and Brave, on Mac, Windows,
Linux and Chromebook. It doesn't work in Safari or Firefox.

1. On this page, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it. On a Mac, double click the ZIP file. On Windows, right click it and choose **Extract All**.
   Put the unzipped folder somewhere you'll keep it, like your Documents folder.
3. Open Chrome's Extensions page: click the three dots in the top right, then **Extensions**, then **Manage
   Extensions**. (In Edge, it's the three dots, **Extensions**, **Manage extensions**.)
4. Turn on **Developer mode**. It's a switch in the top right in Chrome, and on the left side in Edge.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder, the one with `manifest.json` inside.
6. Click the puzzle piece icon in the toolbar and pin **Listing Collector for Etsy** so its icon stays visible.

Chrome sometimes shows a notice about developer mode extensions. That's normal for any extension that didn't
come from the Chrome Web Store, and you can close it.

If you move or rename the folder later, Chrome loses the extension. Load it again from the new spot. Your
saved listings are kept in Chrome's own storage, so they're still there.

## How to use it

- **On a search results page,** each listing gets a row of three save buttons. Click one and it turns orange
  to show the listing is saved there. Click it again to remove it, or click a different one to move it.
  Listings Etsy marks as ads are saved with Ad = Yes.
- **On a single listing page,** a small panel in the bottom right corner has the same three buttons.
- **The lists** start out named Ad research, Ad reference and Product idea. Click the toolbar icon, then
  **Rename lists**, to call them whatever you like.
- **Click the toolbar icon** to see your lists. Each row has the photo, title, shop, price, the date you saved
  it and a note field.
- **Download CSV** saves the list as a spreadsheet file. Google Sheets, Excel and Numbers all open it.
- **Copy for Google Sheets** copies the table so you can paste it into cell A1 of a sheet.

## Privacy Mode, for screen recordings

If you record your Etsy research for a video, Privacy Mode hides who is behind each
listing so you aren't putting other sellers or their buyers on screen.

- **Turn it on:** click the toolbar icon and flip the **Privacy Mode** switch. Open Etsy
  tabs change right away, and the setting is remembered. A small **Privacy Mode On**
  badge sits in the bottom left corner of Etsy pages while it's on.
- **What it hides:** shop names (search results, the "Ad · By" line, listing pages,
  shop pages and any text naming the shop), shop logos and banners, the owner's name
  and photo, reviewer names, reviewer photos and buyer review photos. On a shop page
  the tab title reads "Shop name hidden".
- **What stays:** product photos, titles, prices, sales counts, ratings and badges.
- **It keeps up as you scroll,** including new results, more reviews and review photo
  pop ups.
- **Saving still works.** The save buttons and the CSV still record the real shop name.
  The list in the popup shows "Shop hidden" while Privacy Mode is on.

Two things no extension can hide: the shop name in Chrome's address bar on a shop page
(crop the top of the window out in your video editor), and a viewer who goes looking
for a shop on their own. Hiding names is a courtesy to other sellers, not a lock.

## What gets saved for each listing

List, Title, Listing URL, Shop, Shop URL, Price, Original price (when on sale), Ad, Date added, Note, Search
term, Found on, Listing ID and Image URL.

## Your data

Everything is stored inside your own browser. The extension only runs on etsy.com pages, and it never sends
anything anywhere.

If Etsy changes its page design and the buttons stop appearing, I'll update the extension here.
